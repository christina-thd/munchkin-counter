# Instrument sounds

The note recordings in this folder (nylon guitar, upright bass, taiko drum, piano, bassoon)
come from the
**FluidR3_GM** soundfont, as published note by note by the midi-js-soundfonts project:
https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts

License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Only a note every three semitones is kept; the dashboard's music (js/table/music.js) plays the notes in between
by playing these slightly faster or slower. The tunes themselves are original (js/table/tunes.js).
